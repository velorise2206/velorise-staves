## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q: Why do my staves stop working when I carry too many?
**A:** You have triggered **Elemental Overload**! By default, carrying more than **4 combat staves** in your inventory causes an overload that shuts down all staves until you deposit excess staves into a chest or backpack. This encourages strategic party-building and prevents carrying every single weapon at once.

### Q: How do I activate Elemental Burst [Q]?
**A:** In Minecraft, press the **[R] key** on your keyboard while holding a signature weapon! This unleashes the character's signature Ultimate ability with 1.5s–2.5s of invulnerability frames (I-frames).

### Q: How do I switch elements on the Omni Staff?
**A:** Hold or press the **[Tab] key** to bring up the circular **Omni Element Wheel**. Move your mouse to select an element and release.

### Q: How do I use the Wind Glider and Plunging Attack?
**A:** When falling or in midair, press **[Space]** to deploy the Wings of First Flight. While gliding or falling, **Left-Click** to execute a high-speed Plunging Attack that cancels all fall damage!

### Q: Does Netherite upgrade change the cooldown?
**A:** No. Netherite upgrades increase Damage and Gauge Units by **+50%** and increase range/AoE radius, but weapon cooldowns remain identical.

## Mod Compatibility

| Mod | Compatibility Status | Details |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **JEI / REI / EMI** | **Full Support** | Displays Crafting Table recipes, Anvil Imbuing recipes, and Smithing Table Netherite upgrades. |
| **Jade / Waila** | **Full Support** | Displays overhead mob element auras, exact Gauge Units (1U/2U), and remaining Freeze durations. |
| **First Person Model / Better First Person** | **Full Support** | The mod features dedicated 1st-person render layers and transforms for staves, bows, quills, and vehicles. |
| **Iris / Oculus / Shaders** | **Full Support** | Custom translucent render batches (arcs, spheres, crystallize shards) render cleanly above shader water and terrain. |
| **Sodium / Embeddium / Rubidium** | **Full Support** | Fully optimized batch rendering; high FPS and low memory overhead. |
