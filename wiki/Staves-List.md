## Crafting & Upgrading Guide

- **Elemental Staves:** Crafted in a Crafting Table using the core catalyst (e.g., Fire Charge, Ghast Tear, Blue Ice, Wind Charge, Totem of Undying, etc.) combined diagonally with **2 Blaze Rods** (or **Breeze Rods** / **End Rods** / **Amethyst Shards**).
- **Netherite Upgrade:** Combine **Netherite Upgrade Smithing Template + Basic Staff + Netherite Ingot** in a **Smithing Table** for +50% combat stats and extended range (cooldowns unchanged).
- **Omni Staff Crafting:**
  - *Method 1 (Crafting Table):* Combine **7 Non-Archon Staves** (or Trident for Hydro / Void Translocator for Electro) + **1 Diamond/Netherite Sword**.
  - *Method 2 (Anvil):* Upgrade any Staff + **Omni Element** in an Anvil (preserves Enchantments and Netherite tier), or swap absorbed staves/Trident/Void Translocator in an Anvil.

## Elemental Resonance & Inventory Cap

Carrying staves in your inventory grants team buffs based on elemental compositions (identical to Genshin Impact):

- **Pyro Resonance (Fervent Flames):** +25% Melee Attack DMG; affected by Cryo for 40% less time.
- **Hydro Resonance (Soothing Water):** +25% Max HP; extinguish fire 2x faster; affected by Pyro for 40% less time.
- **Anemo Resonance (Impetuous Winds):** +10% Movement Speed; -15% Stamina/Exhaustion; **-10% Skill & Burst Cooldowns**!
- **Electro Resonance (High Voltage):** Triggering Superconduct, Overloaded, Electro-Charged, Quicken, Aggravate, or Hyperbloom has a 100% chance to generate an Electro Particle (restores 1.0s cooldown to all active staves; 5s ICD).
- **Dendro Resonance (Sprawling Greenery):** +1 Elemental Mastery (EM). After triggering Burning, Bloom, or Quicken: +1 EM for 6s. After triggering Aggravate, Spread, Hyperbloom, or Burgeon: +1 EM for 6s.
- **Cryo Resonance (Shattering Ice):** Against enemies affected by Cryo or Frozen: +15% Critical Chance (+1.5x damage bonus); affected by Electro for 40% less time.
- **Geo Resonance (Enduring Rock):** +15% Shield Strength. When protected by a shield: +15% DMG dealt; dealing damage shreds enemy Geo RES by 20% for 15s.
- **Protective Canopy (4 Unique Elements):** +15% All Elemental & Physical RES.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Staff Inventory Cap:** Carrying more than **4 combat staves** at once triggers **Elemental Overload**, temporarily disabling all staff skills and resonances until excess staves are stored in chests/backpacks (Creative mode exempt).
