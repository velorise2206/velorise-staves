# Staves List (22 Combat Staves)

All staves support **Basic** and **Netherite** tiers (**+50% DMG / +50% Gauge U** and extended range on Netherite) and scale with **Elemental Mastery I–III**.

## Crafting & Upgrading (Text Guide)
- **Elemental Staves:** Crafted in a Crafting Table using the staff's core catalyst (e.g., Fire Charge, Ghast Tear, Blue Ice, Wind Charge, Totem of Undying, etc.) combined diagonally with **2 Blaze Rods** (or **Breeze Rods** / **End Rods** / **Amethyst Shards** depending on the staff).
- **Netherite Upgrade:** Combine **Netherite Upgrade Smithing Template + Basic Staff + Netherite Ingot** in a **Smithing Table** for +50% combat stats and extended range (cooldowns unchanged).
- **Omni Staff Crafting:**
  - *Method 1 (Crafting Table):* Combine **7 Non-Archon Staves** (or Trident for Hydro / Void Translocator for Electro) + **1 Diamond/Netherite Sword**.
  - *Method 2 (Anvil):* Upgrade any Staff + **Omni Element** in an Anvil (preserves Enchantments and Netherite tier), or swap absorbed staves/Trident/Void Translocator in an Anvil.
