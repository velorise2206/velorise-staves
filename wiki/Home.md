# Velorise's Staves & Elements Wiki

Welcome to the official wiki for **Velorise's Staves & Elements** (NeoForge 1.21.1, Fabric 1.21.1, and Forge 1.20.1).

The mod introduces **22 elemental combat staves** (including the 7 Archon signature weapons, 3 Genshin character weapons — Neuvillette, Xilonen, and Layla, plus the Omni Staff), **element-imbued swords**, **8 elemental essences**, **2 utility relics**, and a full **Elemental Gauge Theory (EGT)** reaction system.
